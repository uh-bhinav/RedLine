# TrailLight Gear — Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping (United States)
Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days and costs $6.99, or is free on orders over $75. Expedited shipping (2–3 business days) costs $14.99 and is available at checkout.

## International Shipping
We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. International orders take 10–18 business days to arrive and shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their country.

## Order Tracking
Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to update after the shipping confirmation email is sent.

## Lost or Delayed Packages
If a package shows as delivered but you have not received it, or tracking has not updated for more than 10 business days, contact support so we can open an investigation with the carrier. We are not able to issue refunds for packages confirmed as delivered to the address on file.
