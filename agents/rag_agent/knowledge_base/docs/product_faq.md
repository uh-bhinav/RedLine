# TrailLight Gear — Product FAQ

## Warranty Coverage
All TrailLight backpacks and tents carry a 2-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, or damage from improper storage.

## Sizing Guidance
Our jackets and base layers run true to standard US sizing. For backpacks, we size by torso length rather than overall height — measure from the base of your neck to the top of your hip bones, and use that measurement when choosing a pack size, not your height.

## Care Instructions
Most TrailLight outerwear should be machine washed cold on a gentle cycle with a technical-fabric detergent, then air dried. Do not use standard fabric softener, as it can degrade waterproof (DWR) coatings over time. Tents and packs should be spot-cleaned with a damp cloth only.

## Replacement Parts
Tent pole sections, stuff sacks, and buckle hardware can be ordered individually as spare parts through our support team — they are not sold through the main online store.
