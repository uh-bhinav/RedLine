# TrailLight Gear — Refund & Returns Policy

## Standard Returns Window
You may return any unworn, unwashed item in its original packaging within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund to your original payment method. Items returned after 30 days but within 60 days are eligible for store credit only, not a cash refund.

## How Refunds Are Processed
Once we receive your returned item at our warehouse, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. You will receive an email confirmation when your refund has been issued. Refunds typically appear on your statement within 3–5 additional business days, depending on your bank.

## Non-Returnable Items
Final sale items (marked "Final Sale" on the product page), custom or personalized gear, and gift cards are not eligible for return or refund under any circumstances. Worn or used footwear cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless defective.

## Defective or Damaged Items
If an item arrives defective or damaged, contact support within 14 days of delivery for a free replacement or full refund, including original shipping costs. We will email you a prepaid return label.
